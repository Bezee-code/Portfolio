Upstox MTF Smart Nudge & Risk Mitigation Engine

A customer-facing intervention layer designed to help MTF traders address margin shortfalls efficiently before forced square-off, while deferring to authoritative RMS controls.

⚠️ THE PROBLEM
Off-app SMS alerts lack in-context guidance, causing multi-step navigation friction and avoidable forced square-offs.
💡 THE SOLUTION
In-app Smart Nudge surfacing explicit risk state, shortfall breakdown, and recommended next action (Add Cash | Pledge | Trim).
🎯 PRIMARY PRODUCT METRIC [PROPOSED]
Successful Risk Resolution Rate
"Share of eligible MTF risk events where authoritative shortfall is cleared within the resolution window without forced square-off."
❓ WHY SMART NUDGE? [DECISION HYPOTHESIS]
"If research confirms that customers recognize the margin problem but struggle to decide or execute the next action, a guided intervention layer is a stronger V1 bet than simply increasing notification frequency."

⚖️ 02. REGULATORY & RISK CONTEXT

"Smart Nudge is an intervention layer around authoritative broker/RMS controls, not a replacement for them."

1. MTF Margin / Risk Rules: Consume authoritative margin/risk state from broker RMS. [TO BE VALIDATED WITH RISK/COMPLIANCE]
2. RMS Authority & Liquidation Boundary: Never promise guaranteed resolution windows. Always defer to authoritative RMS state.
3. Pledge Workflow: Surface pledge option only when securities are eligible and CDSL/NSDL T-PIN can be initiated. [TO BE VALIDATED]
4. Regulatory Change Watch: Keep rules configurable for SEBI framework reviews. [PROPOSED / CONSULTATION]
⛔ DOES NOT CONTROL: Exchange formulas, RMS liquidation, haircuts, pledge approvals.
✅ CONTROLS: Risk explanation, option prioritization, guided workflows, metric tracking.
